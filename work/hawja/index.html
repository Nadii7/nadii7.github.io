Local services

Hawja

Hawja helps Arabic speakers find everyday services in Egypt, from Sudanese home cooking to nearby schools. A listing brings ratings, a map and direct contact with the provider into one place.

  • Flutter
  • Provider
  • Dio
  • Paginated search

From a category to a contact

Someone looking for a meal can browse local kitchens, compare ratings and check for a discount. The provider page puts the address, map and contact options together, so the next step can be a call or a WhatsApp message.

How search works

I used Provider to track the selected category and location in Flutter. A separate search repository uses Dio to fetch matching services one page at a time as people browse.
