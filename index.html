About & Expertise

What happens when a learner leaves one video lesson and immediately joins another? For Fluency live, I changed RealtimeKit so the next call waits for the previous one to release its listeners. In Remotely Tasks, I built a camera that lets inspectors retake a photo without leaving their checklist.

I build these flows in Dart, manage state with Riverpod or BLoC, and test them with Flutter widget tests. In Cita Health, a single Freezed model keeps the patient, appointment time and insurance together as a booking changes.
